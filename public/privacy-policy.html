Privacy Policy

This policy describes information handled by the YoMart shopping app and website.

Information we handle

  • Account information: username, email address, password hash and account timestamps.
  • Shopping and customer support information: cart and favorites stored on your device, reviews, orders, selected payment method and delivery/contact details you submit.
  • Delivery location: approximate coordinates only if you choose a location on the delivery map and confirm it.
  • Images you submit: payment receipts and other supported images. Payment proof is stored privately and is not exposed through public order responses.
  • Technical request data: hosting and API providers may process IP address, request time and device/browser information in service logs.
  • Push notifications are not enabled in the current release. If this changes, this policy must be updated before release.

How we use information

Information is used to create and secure accounts, display the catalog, process physical-product orders, coordinate collection or handover, handle payment evidence where applicable, support customers and prevent abuse.

Service providers

The API is hosted by Render. The application database is hosted by MongoDB Atlas. Product, category, poster and private payment-proof images are stored with Cloudinary. Map tiles may be requested from OpenStreetMap tile services when you use the location picker. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

YoMart does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or to protect the service and its users. The owner must approve this statement before publication.

Retention and account deletion

You can request account deletion in the app or through the external account deletion page. Deletion disables sign-in and removes direct profile/contact/delivery identifiers and reviews. Some order and payment evidence may be retained in pseudonymized form for business records and reconciliation; deleted customers lose access to their order history and receipts. The owner must approve the exact retention periods and applicable legal requirements before this policy is finalized.

Security and children

Passwords are stored as salted password hashes and protected API requests require an access token. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. YoMart is a general shopping service and is not designed for children under 13.

Your choices

You may choose whether to provide optional map location information. You can request account deletion using the methods above. Contact YoMart using the final support details below once they have been supplied by the owner.
