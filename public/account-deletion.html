Delete your YoMart account

This page lets you request deletion of your YoMart customer account without reinstalling the app.

  • Sign-in is disabled and direct profile/contact/delivery identifiers are removed.
  • Reviews are removed.
  • Some order and payment evidence may be retained in pseudonymized form for business records and reconciliation.
  • You lose access to order history and payment receipts.

Draft: the owner must approve exact retention periods and legal wording before public launch. Account deletion cannot be undone.
