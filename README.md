

## Current staging catalogue and screenshot review

The connected `yomart_staging` database currently contains 4 categories, 4 subcategories, 2 brands, 8 active synthetic sample products (plus 2 inactive historical test fixtures), 2 curated collections, and 2 campaign posters. Product prices and stock are illustrative test values; all products remain unverified for public search. The staging store enables cash on collection only, requires no proof, and has a zero delivery fee. No payment gateway is configured. Do not treat sample prices as offers or submit real payment details during closed testing.

Use the [staging catalogue and screenshot evidence guide](https://github.com/kalaabalb/yomart-docs/blob/main/docs/launch/staging-catalog-and-screenshot-evidence.md) for the capture checklist. Capture real storefront screenshots on desktop and mobile for home/posters, catalog, product details, cart, checkout and empty/error states. Redact all customer information before sharing.
